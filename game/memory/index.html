Memory Card Match

Flip two cards at a time to find matching animal pairs with as few moves as possible.

How to Play:

  • Click a card to reveal its hidden symbol.
  • Click a second card to see if it matches the first.
  • If they match, both stay face up. If not, they flip back over.
  • Match all 6 pairs to win the game!
