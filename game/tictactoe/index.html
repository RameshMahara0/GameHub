Tic Tac Toe

Align 3 of your marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally before your opponent does.

How to Play:

  • Played on a 3x3 grid.
  • Players alternate turns placing X or O.
  • First to align 3 marks wins.
  • If all 9 cells fill up with no winner, it ends in a draw.
